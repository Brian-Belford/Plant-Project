Meet the stomata

Stomata are tiny pores, usually most numerous on the lower surface of a leaf. Guard cells control whether each pore is open or closed.

How chlorophyll handles light

Inside chloroplasts, chlorophyll is a green pigment in the thylakoid membranes. Test different colors of visible light.

Light absorbed by chlorophyll: Choose a color

Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light strongly. It reflects more green light, helping leaves look green.
